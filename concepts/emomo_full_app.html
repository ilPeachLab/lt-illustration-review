System · Rule 3

The presence budget: full-body is earned, not default

The fastest way to kill a mascot is to show all of it, everywhere, forever, and the category evidence is unanimous. Duolingo keeps full-body Duo out of lessons (roughly 90% of time in app) and spent 18 months building a separate world-characters cast precisely so the mascot wouldn't carry content; in-app animation fatigue still forced them to ship a disable toggle. Finch, the closest analog, is full-size only on its home nest and is absent from its own insights and analytics screens. Headspace strips characters out of sessions entirely so content and user intent stay centered, illustration density decreases as the user goes deeper. Pokémon Sleep is punished in reviews daily for forcing character ceremony on tired users at bedtime and waking, and this product's entire audience is tired parents. So: Emomo appears at full size only when it is actually talking with Sari. On browsing surfaces it is at most a peek. On record, health, and reading surfaces it is the dock face and nothing more. Absence is the proof the memory-keeper knows when to be quiet. ADR-005.1 in visual form. This table is the enforcement artifact; every frame in this document must be able to point at its row.

System · Health

The health half: same grammar, quieter clothes

Measurements, vaccines, sleep, and the check-up ritual join the app without adding a single new input surface. You still just tell Emomo. These four principles govern the Care chapter; the frames that render them arrive with Parts 4–7.

One intake

Everything enters through the same door: tell Emomo. "She's 68 cm now" or "second dose done today" gets parsed, then structured-confirmed. Emomo repeats what it understood as a tidy card ("68 cm, measured today, right?") before it lands in the record. No forms-first health tab.

Two chapters

Mei's Book has two chapters: Firsts (development, pebbles, chapters, seasons) and Care (health, growth, vaccines, sleep, visits). One book, one owner, one tear-out for the doctor. The Care door in the dock is the fast path into this chapter, health gets checked in a hurry. Care never leaks celebration mechanics; Firsts never leaks clinical framing.

The check-up heartbeat

Health's rhythm is the visit: a gentle countdown ("Dr. Chan, in 12 days"), a "what we're bringing" review (the visit list + the tear-out page), and a post-visit debrief where Emomo asks how it went and files what the doctor said. Emomo carries the bag; Sari walks in prepared.

Calm on screen, clinical on paper

Clinical status colors exist only on the doctor document. On screen, care items are due "soon" or "at the next visit" in the same warm vocabulary as everything else. Emomo never says "overdue." The paper the pediatrician holds can be precise; the screen the parent holds stays kind.

C10 · ASK ABOUT THIS

A bounded thread that always ends in an action

New affordance on any kept moment: "Ask about this" opens a short thread anchored to the observation. The anchor chip at the top is the contract, this conversation is about that moment, with Mei's record as its context. Every thread terminates in the same four-chip row, keep it, try a game, add to visit list, done, equal dignity, no dead ends; the composer disappears once the thread resolves, which is the whole argument in one control. Emomo runs listening and gentle-care registers only, no proud face, no celebration, this surface keeps, it never grades.

Why bounded, not open: an open chat about your baby drifts into daily diagnostic questions, exactly the logging-tool-versus-clinical-tool line ADR-005.1 exists to hold; and the beta's own test says a companion interaction that ends in nothing is novelty, not value. Bounding by intent and terminating in an action answers both. It is also the differentiation and the cost model: ChatGPT doesn't know Mei's record, and here every conversation ends in something kept, tried, or shared; a small model routes intent, the large model speaks only on education turns, and the chip row caps thread length. Instrument per-intent from day one; the failure signal is threads that end with no action taken.
